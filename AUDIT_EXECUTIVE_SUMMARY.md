## 3 BIGGEST OPPORTUNITIES (Ranked by Impact)

### 🔴 CRITICAL: Post-Calculator User Journey (6.5/10)
**The Problem:** Users enter calculator, get result, see generic "Next tools" link, then leave.

**Impact:** Users aren't exploring cross-tools, so session depth = 1-2 pages (low ad impressions, missed opportunities).

**Estimated Fix:**
- Add "Next Steps" recommendation engine after results (based on result interpretation)
- Estimated uplift: +25-30% multi-tool engagement, +20% time-on-site, +25% ad revenue

**Effort:** 5 days | **Priority:** WEEK 1

---

### 🟠 HIGH: Monetization Channel Diversification (5.5/10)
**The Problem:** Site relies 100% on AdSense. True potential is much higher.

**Hidden Revenue:**
- Affiliate partnerships (Shopify Experts, Mixpanel, analytics tools): +$500-1,500/month
- B2B lead generation (finance audiences, agency consultants): +$500-2,000/month
- Premium features (advanced calculators, historical tracking): +$5-15K/month at scale

**Estimated Fix:**
- Add 5-8 affiliate partnerships (in content recommendations)
- Create "Connect with Expert" B2B lead gen workflow
- MVP premium tier ($9/month with advanced features)

**Effort:** 18 days | **Priority:** WEEKS 2-8

---

### 🟡 MEDIUM: SEO Coverage Gaps (7.5/10)
